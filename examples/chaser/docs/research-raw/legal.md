## Contradictions and gaps
- DPDP dates: 13 vs 14 Nov/May by source. The 12-month compression is proposed, not confirmed notified. The S.O. 5458(E) claim is unverified and could change timing.
- Section 7(a) vs notice: commentators disagree whether invoice data qualifies. Nothing says the freelancer-to-client notice can be skipped.
- Not found: DPDP text on processor liability; GST invoice rules (judged out of scope, as Chaser doesn't issue invoices); India's Telecom Act 2023 spam provisions; WhatsApp policy on debt-collection content; any case of an "invoice chaser" being banned; NPCI position on displaying UPI IDs; TRAI category for freelancer reminders.
- Pro-product evidence: personal-number automation is the biggest disconfirming finding (rows 7-8). No evidence the category is illegal, but also no evidence it is cleared.
