Privacy and terms

Last updated 20 October 2026. This is a plain-language notice, not legal advice.

What Tide stores

Only on your own device, in your browser's local storage: the three session lengths you chose, whether sound is on, and how many focus sessions you finished today, with today's date so the count starts again tomorrow. Nothing else. You can remove it any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

What Tide does not do

  • It has no accounts and asks for no name or email.
  • It sets no cookies and has no analytics, advertising or third-party scripts, fonts or images.
  • After the page has loaded it makes no requests at all. Your timer runs entirely in your browser.
  • It sends no notifications.

What the host sees

Tide is served by a web host (Vercel). Like any web host, it can keep ordinary access logs of requests for the page's files, such as your IP address, the time and your browser type. Tide cannot see them and does not use them. The host's own privacy policy applies to those logs.
